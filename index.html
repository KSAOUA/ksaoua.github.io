FAQ

What do I get after paying?

An .xlsx file you can download immediately from the order confirmation page and from the receipt email. It is yours to keep; there is no subscription.

Which apps are supported?

Excel 2016 or newer and Microsoft 365 on Windows. Excel for Mac, Numbers and LibreOffice are not tested. The templates use standard formulas only: no macros and no add-ins.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Not tested yet. The templates use standard formulas, but we only promise Excel for now.

Is this financial or tax advice?

No. These are tools for organizing your own numbers. For tax questions, ask a local accountant.

Can I use it for my business or my clients?

Yes. One purchase covers use inside your own household or business, including reports you make for your clients. Reselling or sharing the template file itself is not allowed. See the terms of use.

What if it does not work for me?

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. The templates are ordinary offline spreadsheet files. Your data stays with you.
