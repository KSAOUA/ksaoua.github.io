Which Excel versions are supported?

Excel 2016 or newer on Windows, Excel for Mac and Microsoft 365. The templates use standard formulas only: no macros and no add-ins.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

The templates are built and tested for Excel. Most formulas also work in Google Sheets, but charts and formatting can look different, so we do not promise full compatibility.

Can I use it for my company or my clients?

Yes. One purchase covers use inside your own business, including reports you make for your clients. Reselling or sharing the template file itself is not allowed. See the terms of use.

What if it does not work for me?

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. The templates are ordinary offline Excel files. Your data stays on your computer.
